A QUICK START

Sudoku, in a nutshell.

No math, no guesswork required. Just a little noticing, one number at a time.

01

Know the grid

A Sudoku board has 9 rows and 9 columns, split into nine smaller 3×3 boxes. Some numbers are already filled in as clues.

02

Use each number once

Fill every empty cell with a number from 1 to 9. Each number can appear only once in any row, column, or 3×3 box.

03

Look for what’s missing

Choose an empty cell and check its row, column, and box. Any number already present in one of them can’t go in that cell.

04

Try a different shape

Jigsaw Sudoku follows the same rules, except the nine 3×3 boxes are replaced by nine irregular regions. Each region still needs one of every number.
